Flagship Product

Benim Hafızım

A practical companion for Qur'an reading, listening, memorization, and progress tracking.

Google Play (In Review) App Store (Coming Soon)
Designed for Every Reader: Benim Hafızım is built for anyone seeking a structured, peaceful routine with the Qur'an — whether you read a page every morning, listen during your daily commute, memorize selected surahs, or manage an intensive revision schedule.

Reading & Continuous Tracking

Crystal-clear typography and distraction-free Mushaf rendering that automatically bookmarks your exact page, ayah, and reading history.

Surah Audio & Custom Playlists

High-quality recitations paired with the unique ability to build personalized audio playlists for focused study, reflection, or travel.

Memorization & Retention Tracking

Systematic revision tools that organize memorized portions into scheduled reviews, helping prevent forgetting and cementing progress.

Personal Goals & Habit Building

Set realistic daily reading or recitation targets. Gentle indicators keep your streaks active and your long-term connection consistent.

Clear Progress Visualization

Honest, calm visual metrics showing your reading frequency, completed chapters, and memorization milestones without noisy gamification.

Unified All-in-One Experience

Reading, audio playback, personal notes, and memorization tracking united under a single, fast, distraction-free native mobile interface.

About the Company

An independent studio focused on long-term utility.

WestOtto is an independent, early-stage mobile technology startup. We build software for individuals who appreciate craftsmanship, clarity, and dependable software that respects their time.

Too much of modern mobile software is weighed down by excessive notifications, bloated feature sets, and manipulative engagement loops. At WestOtto, we take the opposite path: we design focused products that do their job exceptionally well, launch quickly, work reliably offline, and stay out of the user's way.

From habit companions like Benim Hafızım to media utilities like Do Podcast and Download X, our commitment is simple: build products we are proud to use ourselves every single day.

Intentional Restraint

Every feature, button, and visual element must justify its existence. We prioritize clean workflows over unnecessary complexity.

User Privacy & Trust

We build with an offline-first architecture whenever possible, minimizing data collection and ensuring personal routines remain private.

High-Performance Engineering

Applications are built using modern cross-platform native frameworks, ensuring instant startup times, smooth 60fps animations, and low battery consumption.

AI-Augmented Engineering (Powered by Claude)

We leverage Anthropic's Claude as our core architectural pair-programmer — accelerating database schema verification, clean architecture design, and production-grade mobile engineering at 10x velocity without sacrificing code craftsmanship.
